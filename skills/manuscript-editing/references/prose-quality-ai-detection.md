# Scientific Prose Quality

Use this guide to revise observable prose problems. Do not infer whether a passage was written by a human or an AI; authorship cannot be established reliably from stylistic features.

## Diagnose the function, not the source

Revise wording when it is vague, repetitive, inflated, formulaic, or logically weak. Terms sometimes associated with generic generated prose—such as “delve,” “landscape,” “underscore,” or stacked transitions—are not inherently wrong. Replace them only when a more precise expression improves the sentence.

Prefer:

- a concrete subject and action over abstract nominalizations;
- the specific result over “these findings” when reference is ambiguous;
- direct logical connections over decorative transitions;
- calibrated verbs such as “was associated with,” “reduced,” or “supports” over promotional language;
- sentence-length and structure variation that follows the argument rather than an arbitrary rhythm.

## Scientific precision

Use quantitative detail only when it appears in the supplied source. If precision is needed but absent, insert a placeholder or ask for it. Never create a sample size, percentage, effect estimate, p-value, confidence interval, test name, or adverse-event rate to make prose seem more scientific.

Preserve the evidentiary distinction among observation, association, prediction, mechanism, and causation.

## Examples

### Remove vague promotional language

Original:

> Drug X worked and may be a game-changer.

Revision when no quantitative results were supplied:

> The reported findings suggest that drug X warrants further evaluation.

If the evidence level is unclear, retain uncertainty or use a placeholder rather than supplying invented results.

### Improve flow without adding facts

Original:

> Protein levels were measured in tissue samples. The measurements showed differences. The differences were analyzed statistically.

Revision:

> Protein levels were measured in tissue samples, and the observed differences were evaluated statistically.

This revision changes structure only; it does not add groups, effect sizes, tests, or significance claims.

### Replace empty framing

Original:

> It is important to note that this finding underscores the complex landscape of protein interactions.

Revision:

> This finding indicates that the measured protein interactions vary across the studied conditions.

Use this version only if variation across conditions is actually established by the source.

## Final check

Compare the revision with the source and remove any detail that came from stylistic inference rather than supplied evidence. Clarity is not permission to manufacture specificity.
