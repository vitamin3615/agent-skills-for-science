# Scientific Collocations for CNS-Quality Writing

## Purpose
This reference provides a curated list of high-impact scientific word pairings (collocations) derived from analysis of published *Cell*, *Nature*, and *Science* papers.

**How to Use:**
1. **Diagnosis:** Identify vague or weak phrasing in your draft (e.g., "big change," "strongly affects").
2. **Selection:** Consult the relevant category below to find a precise, density-enhancing alternative (e.g., "robustly induced," "profoundly altered").
3. **Verification:** Ensure the strength of the collocation matches your evidence (see `word-level-precision.md`).

---

## 1. Verbs of Causality & Regulation

**Instead of:** "affects," "changes," "influences"

| Context | High-Value Collocations | Evidence Level |
|---------|-------------------------|----------------|
| **Strong Induction** | **robustly induces**, **potently activates**, **triggers a cascade of**, **drive the expression of**, **elicits a profound**, **primes the response**, **potentiates**, **stimulates**, **fuels**, **promotes**, **activates**, **initiates and propagates** | Gain-of-function |
| **Strong Suppression** | **abrogates**, **abolishes**, **blunts**, **compromises**, **attenuates**, **restricts**, **impedes**, **suppresses unwanted**, **dampens**, **antagonizes**, **prohibits**, **limits the availability**, **inactivating**, **mitigating** | Loss-of-function |
| **Necessity** | **is indispensable for**, **is required for**, **governs**, **dictates**, **is essential for**, **necessitates**, **relies on**, **hinges on** | KO/KD data |
| **Sufficiency** | **is sufficient to**, **recapitulates the phenotype**, **can uniquely drive**, **confers**, **fosters** | Overexpression |
| **Modulation** | **finely tunes**, **calibrates**, **orchestrates**, **modulates**, **shapes**, **wires**, **tilts the balance**, **reconfigures**, **reshapes**, **rewires** | Subtle changes |
| **Mechanism** | **mechanistically links**, **underpins**, **mediates**, **serves as a checkpoint**, **unleash the ability**, **bridges the gap**, **catalyzes the conjugation**, **exemplifies the interplay**, **elucidate the mechanisms**, **underlie cell-state transitions** | Mechanistic proof |
