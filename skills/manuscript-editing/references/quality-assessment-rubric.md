# Phase 0: Manuscript Quality Assessment Rubric

**Purpose:** BEFORE any editing, assess the original manuscript to determine appropriate editing intensity.

---

## Step 1: Evaluate Original Prose Quality

Rate each dimension 1-5 (1 = poor, 5 = excellent):

| Dimension              | 1 (Poor)                          | 3 (Adequate)                 | 5 (Excellent)                 |
| ---------------------- | --------------------------------- | ---------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| **Sentence Clarity**   | Confusing, requires re-reading    | Understandable but awkward   | Clear, flows naturally        |
| **Word Choice**        | Vague, imprecise, or incorrect    | Acceptable but could improve | Precise, effective            |
| **Scientific English** | Non-standard, many grammar issues | Minor issues, mostly correct | Native-level, polished        |
| **Logical Flow**       | Disjointed, hard to follow        | Adequate connections         | Smooth, compelling narrative  |
| **Conciseness**        | Verbose, redundant                | Some wordiness               | Economical, every word counts |

### Calculate Average Score → Editing Mode

| Average Score | Editing Mode | Word Change Range | Focus                                               |
| ------------- | ------------ | ----------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| **4.0-5.0**   | Light        | <30%              | Add precision, fix specific problems                |
| **2.5-3.9**   | Standard     | 30-60%            | Improve clarity while preserving effective elements |
| **1.0-2.4**   | Intensive    | 60-90%            | Substantial rewriting to achieve publishability     |

---

## Step 1.5: Assess Prose Source (Detect AI-Generated Patterns)

**Scan for AI-isms:**

- Verb overuse ("delve," "utilize," "leverage")
- Abstract metaphors ("tapestry of," "rich landscape of")
- Robotic transitions ("Furthermore," "Moreover," "Additionally" in sequence)
- Sentence monotony (same structure repeated)
- Generic phrasing lacking specificity

**Prose classification:** Human-authored | AI-assisted | Hybrid

**If AI-assisted:** Apply additional refinement strategies from [prose-quality-ai-detection.md](prose-quality-ai-detection.md):

- AI-ism removal
- Phrase bank consultation
- Sentence structure variation

---

## Step 2: Identify Author Context (if known)

| Author Context                                                      | Recommended Mode      |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------- |
| Native English speaker with good writing training                   | Light or Standard     |
| Non-native speaker or ESL author                                    | Standard or Intensive |
| Author explicitly requests "heavy editing" or "rewrite for clarity" | Intensive             |
| Author requests "light touch" or "preserve my style"                | Light                 |

---

## Step 3: Document Your Assessment

Before editing, create this note:

```
Quality Assessment:
- Prose quality score: [X.X/5.0]
- Editing mode: [Light/Standard/Intensive]
- Prose source: [Human-authored/AI-assisted/Hybrid]
- Key issues: [list 2-3 main problems]
- Author context: [if known]
```

---

## Step 4: Calibrate Expectations

| Mode          | Expected Outcome         | Word Change Range | Primary Focus                                |
| ------------- | ------------------------ | ----------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| **Light**     | Polish good writing      | <30%              | Add precision, fix specific issues           |
| **Standard**  | Improve adequate writing | 30-60%            | Enhance clarity and flow                     |
| **Intensive** | Transform poor writing   | 60-90%            | Substantial restructuring for publishability |

---

## Critical Reminders

1. **In Intensive mode:** Extensive changes are EXPECTED and APPROPRIATE. Do not self-censor needed improvements.

2. **ESL Author Consideration:** For manuscripts by non-native English speakers:

   - Original "voice" often includes non-native patterns that obscure meaning
   - Authors typically WANT substantial prose revision
   - Focus on clarity and standard scientific English, not preserving non-native patterns

3. **The Goal:** PUBLISHABILITY at CNS level, not minimal changes.

---

## During-Editing Intensity Check

| Your Mode     | Expected Changes | If Exceeding                                     |
| ------------- | ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| **Light**     | <30%             | Review: Are changes truly necessary?             |
| **Standard**  | 30-60%           | Normal range—continue                            |
| **Intensive** | 60-90%           | Expected for poor originals—continue confidently |

**If changes exceed expected range:** Review each change for value, but do NOT automatically revert. Extensive changes may be exactly what the manuscript needs.
