# Figure Legends-Specific Editing Guidelines

**Purpose:** Provide self-contained, complete descriptions of all figure panels enabling understanding without reading main text.

**Key principle:** Figure legends must be interpretable independently—readers should understand the data without consulting Methods or Results.

---

## Critical Requirements for Figure Legends

### 1. The Figure Title (The "Takeaway")

**Format:** Bolded, first sentence of the legend.

**Rule:** Must be a complete, declarative sentence stating the primary conclusion of the figure.

**❌ BAD (Descriptive/Topic labels):**
- "Figure 1. Analysis of AT2 cells in aged mice."
- "Figure 2. NUPR1 expression data."
- "Figure 3. Survival curves and Western blots."

**✅ GOOD (Conclusive/Active):**
- "Figure 1. **Aged AT2 cells exhibit reduced cell-intrinsic potential for regeneration.**"
- "Figure 2. **Induction of Nupr1 suppresses tumorigenesis in the aged lung by disrupting iron homeostasis.**"
- "Figure 3. **NUPR1 drives functional iron insufficiency and ferroptosis resistance.**"

**Key attributes:**
- **Active Voice:** "X drives Y" (not "Y is driven by X")
- **Specific:** Name the key molecules, cell types, or mechanisms.
- **Directional:** Use verbs like "suppresses", "promotes", "limits", "drives", "orchestrates".

---

### 2. Self-Contained Descriptions

**Must include ALL information needed to interpret figure:**
- What is shown (assay type, measurement)
- What experimental system (cells, mice, tissue)
- What treatment/condition
- What is being compared
- Sample size (n)
- Statistical information
- Technical specifications (scale bars, staining, etc.)

**Test:** Could a reader understand this figure without reading the paper?
- ✅ YES = good legend
- ❌ NO = incomplete legend

---

### 2. Panel Identification (MANDATORY Format)

**Format:** Use letter, comma, space, then description
- ✅ **"a, Description of panel a. b, Description of panel b. c, Description."**
- ❌ NOT: "Panel A shows..." or "(A) Description" or "a) Description"

**Each panel gets complete description:**
- What is shown
- Sample size
- Statistics (if quantitative)
- Technical details

**Example (complete multi-panel legend):**
> **Figure 3. SUV39H1 protein levels increase with age.**
> **a,** Representative Western blot showing SUV39H1 and β-actin (loading control) in young (3-month) and aged (20-month) AT2 cells. **b,** Quantification of SUV39H1 band intensity normalized to β-actin (n = 8 biological replicates; **P** = 0.003, two-tailed Student's t-test; data show mean ± s.d.). **c,** Representative immunofluorescence images of SUV39H1 (green) and DAPI (blue) in young and aged AT2 cells. Scale bar, 20 μm. **d,** Quantification of SUV39H1 nuclear fluorescence intensity (n = 150 cells per condition from three independent experiments; **P** < 0.001, Mann–Whitney test; box plots show median with interquartile range).

**Note:** Each panel (a, b, c, d) has complete description with all necessary details.

---

### 3. Statistical Notation (CRITICAL for Quantitative Panels)

**For every quantification panel, include:**

**Sample size (n):**
- "n = 6 mice"
- "n = 8 biological replicates"
- "n = 150 cells per condition from three independent experiments"
- "n = 3 independent experiments"

**P values or significance indicators:**
- Exact P values: "**P** = 0.032"
- Ranges: "**P** < 0.001"
- Significance stars explained: "* **P** < 0.05, ** **P** < 0.01, *** **P** < 0.001"

**Statistical test:**
- "two-tailed Student's t-test"
- "one-way ANOVA with Tukey's post hoc test"
- "Mann–Whitney test"
- "log-rank test"

**Error bars defined:**
- "Data show mean ± s.d."
- "Data show mean ± s.e.m."
- "Box plots show median (center line), 25th-75th percentiles (box), and min-max (whiskers)"
- "Error bars represent s.d."

**Replication specified:**
- "Representative of three independent experiments"
- "n = 3 independent experiments with similar results"
- "All experiments replicated at least twice with similar findings"

**Example (complete statistical notation):**
> **b,** Tumor number in aged vs. young KP mice at 12 weeks after tumor initiation (n = 8 mice per group; **P** = 0.004, two-tailed Student's t-test; data show mean ± s.d.).

---

### 4. Technical Specifications

**For microscopy images:**

**Scale bars (MANDATORY):**
- "Scale bar, 50 μm"
- "Scale bars, 20 μm (main images), 5 μm (insets)"

**Staining/markers identified:**
- "Green, GFP; blue, DAPI"
- "Red, SUV39H1; green, Ki67; blue, nuclei (DAPI)"
- "Arrowheads indicate positive cells"

**Image type specified:**
- "Representative confocal images"
- "Maximum intensity projection of z-stack"
- "Single optical section"

**Magnification (optional but helpful):**
- "63× magnification"
- "40× objective"

**Example (complete technical specs):**
> **c,** Representative confocal images of SUV39H1 (green), Ki67 (red), and DAPI (blue) in young and aged AT2 cells. Arrowheads indicate SUV39H1+Ki67+ double-positive cells. Scale bar, 20 μm. Images are maximum intensity projections of z-stacks acquired at 0.5-μm intervals.

---

**For Western blots:**

**Loading control specified:**
- "β-actin, loading control"
- "GAPDH, loading control"
- "Ponceau S staining, loading control"

**Molecular weight markers (optional):**
- "Numbers indicate molecular weight in kDa"

**Example:**
> **a,** Representative Western blot showing SUV39H1 and β-actin (loading control) in young and aged AT2 cells.

---

**For graphs/plots:**

**Axes labeled in legend or figure:**
- If not obvious from figure: "x axis, time (h); y axis, cell viability (%)"

**Data representation:**
- "Individual data points shown with mean ± s.d."
- "Each symbol represents one biological replicate"

---

### 5. Image Type: Representative vs. Quantification

**Clarify what type of data is shown:**

**Representative images:**
- "Representative images of three independent experiments"
- "Representative Western blot (n = 8 biological replicates)"

**Quantification:**
- "Quantification of SUV39H1 band intensity"
- "Percentage of Ki67-positive cells"

**Why this matters:**
- Representative = shows one example
- Quantification = shows all replicates/measurements

**Example:**
> **a,** Representative Western blot showing SUV39H1 and β-actin. **b,** Quantification of SUV39H1 band intensity from n = 8 biological replicates shown in (a).

---

### 6. Experimental Details (Essential Context)

**For each panel, specify:**

**Cell type/tissue:**
- "in AT2 cells"
- "in lung tissue sections"
- "in isolated alveolar cells"

**Treatment/condition:**
- "treated with deferoxamine (100 μM, 24 h)"
- "aged (20-month) vs. young (3-month) mice"
- "with or without NUPR1 knockdown"

**Time points:**
- "at 12 weeks after tumor initiation"
- "24 h after treatment"
- "over a 72-h time course"

**Example (complete context):**
> **d,** Tumor volume in aged (20-month) and young (3-month) KP mice at 12 weeks after AdCre-mediated tumor initiation (n = 10 mice per group; **P** = 0.012, Mann–Whitney test; box plots show median with interquartile range).

---

## Figure Legend Editing Checklist

**Structure:**
- [ ] Title summarizes main finding
- [ ] Each panel (a, b, c...) described separately
- [ ] Format: "a, Description. b, Description."
- [ ] Complete description for every panel

**Self-Contained:**
- [ ] Can be understood without main text
- [ ] All experimental context provided
- [ ] Assay types specified
- [ ] Treatments/conditions described

**Statistical Notation (for quantitative panels):**
- [ ] Sample sizes (n = X) with units
- [ ] P values or significance indicators
- [ ] Statistical tests named
- [ ] Error bars defined (s.d., s.e.m., etc.)
- [ ] Replication specified

**Technical Specifications:**
- [ ] Scale bars specified for all microscopy images
- [ ] Staining/markers identified (colors)
- [ ] Loading controls specified for Western blots
- [ ] Image type clarified (representative vs. quantification)

**Experimental Details:**
- [ ] Cell type/tissue specified
- [ ] Treatment/condition described
- [ ] Time points stated
- [ ] Genotypes/strains identified

## Figure Legend Templates

### Western Blot + Quantification

> **Figure X. [Protein] levels [increase/decrease] with [condition].**
> **a,** Representative Western blot showing [protein] and [loading control] in [conditions]. **b,** Quantification of [protein] band intensity normalized to [loading control] (n = X biological replicates; **P** = X.XXX, two-tailed Student's t-test; data show mean ± s.d.).

---

### Immunofluorescence + Quantification

> **Figure X. [Marker] expression [increases/decreases] in [condition].**
> **a,** Representative confocal images of [marker 1] ([color]) and [marker 2] ([color]) in [cell type/tissue]. Scale bar, X μm. **b,** Quantification of [marker]-positive cells (n = X cells per condition from Y independent experiments; **P** = X.XXX, Mann–Whitney test; data show mean ± s.d.).

---

### Tumor Measurements

> **Figure X. Aging suppresses tumorigenesis.**
> **a,** Tumor number in [condition 1] vs. [condition 2] mice at X weeks after tumor initiation (n = X mice per group; **P** = X.XXX, two-tailed Student's t-test; data show mean ± s.d.). **b,** Tumor volume measured by [method] (n = X mice per group; **P** = X.XXX, Mann–Whitney test; box plots show median with interquartile range).

---

### Survival Curves

> **Figure X. [Treatment/condition] improves survival.**
> Kaplan–Meier survival curves for [condition 1] and [condition 2] mice (n = X mice per group; **P** = X.XXX, log-rank test).

---

### Multi-Panel Complex Figure

> **Figure X. [Overall finding].**
> **a,** [Description of panel a with experimental details]. **b,** [Quantification of panel a] (n = X; **P** = X.XXX, [test]; data show mean ± s.d.). **c,** [Description of panel c]. Scale bar, X μm. **d,** [Quantification related to panel c] (n = X cells from Y experiments; **P** = X.XXX, [test]; box plots show median with interquartile range). **e,** [Mechanistic validation experiment] (n = X; **P** = X.XXX, [test]; data show mean ± s.e.m.). Representative of Z independent experiments.

## Figure Legend Quality Standards (CNS-Level)

**Gate 1: Completeness**
- [ ] All panels described (a, b, c...)
- [ ] Self-contained (no need to read main text)
- [ ] Experimental context complete

**Gate 2: Statistical Rigor**
- [ ] n values for all quantification
- [ ] P values or significance shown
- [ ] Statistical tests named
- [ ] Error bars defined

**Gate 3: Technical Clarity**
- [ ] Scale bars specified
- [ ] Staining/markers identified
- [ ] Loading controls noted
- [ ] Image types clarified

**Gate 4: Reproducibility**
- [ ] Replication stated
- [ ] Conditions/treatments detailed
- [ ] Time points specified
- [ ] Genotypes/strains identified

## Example Complete Figure Legend

**Figure 3. Aging increases SUV39H1 and H3K9me3 levels in AT2 cells.**

**a,** Representative Western blot showing SUV39H1, H3K9me3, and β-actin (loading control) in young (3-month) and aged (20-month) AT2 cells isolated from KP mice. **b,** Quantification of SUV39H1 band intensity normalized to β-actin (n = 8 biological replicates; **P** = 0.003, two-tailed Student's t-test; data show mean ± s.d.). **c,** Quantification of H3K9me3 band intensity normalized to β-actin (n = 8 biological replicates; **P** = 0.008, two-tailed Student's t-test; data show mean ± s.d.). **d,** Representative confocal images of SUV39H1 (green), H3K9me3 (red), and DAPI (blue) in young and aged AT2 cells. Scale bar, 20 μm. **e,** Quantification of SUV39H1 nuclear fluorescence intensity (n = 150 cells per condition from three independent experiments; **P** < 0.001, Mann–Whitney test; box plots show median, 25th-75th percentiles, and min-max). **f,** Quantification of H3K9me3 nuclear fluorescence intensity (n = 150 cells per condition from three independent experiments; **P** < 0.001, Mann–Whitney test; box plots show median with interquartile range).

**Why this is complete:**
- ✅ All six panels (a-f) described
- ✅ Complete statistical notation (n, P, tests, error bars)
- ✅ Technical specifications (scale bar, staining colors)
- ✅ Experimental context (young vs. aged, cell type, genotype)
- ✅ Image type clarified (representative vs. quantification)
- ✅ Replication specified
- ✅ Self-contained (interpretable without main text)
